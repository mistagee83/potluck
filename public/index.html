How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a project

    Start from a classroom-tested template, or fill in a short form with your own idea. Every project has the same parts: phases, milestones, roles, tasks and assessment criteria.

  2. 2

    Fit it to your class

    Give your subjects, groups, time, level and language. AI rewrites the plan, the tasks and the criteria step by step, and you approve each step. Or run the template as it is.

  3. 3

    Run it on one board

    Students join with a code and a nickname. Groups claim tasks and hand in links to their work. You see who is late and who did what. At the end, students assess themselves and each other, and you give the final scores.

What students see

Students see their group's board on their phone. It shows the tasks of the current phase, who took which one, and the hand-in for the milestone. They don't give an e-mail address or a password, and they don't talk to an AI. If someone joins from a new phone, you approve it first, so nobody can tick tasks in someone else's name.

Questions

Do my students need accounts?

No. They type the project code and a nickname. Their phone stays signed in. If they switch to a new device, you approve it first.

What happens to student data?

Potluck stores nicknames, task status, links to the work and the assessment answers. It doesn't store e-mail addresses or files. Student data is deleted 90 days after you close the project. You keep the plan.

Do students use AI in Potluck?

No. AI works only for you: when you fit a project to your class and, if you want, for written feedback at the end, which you edit before students see it.

What do students hand in?

A link to work made anywhere: a shared document, slides, a video, a map, a Canva page. With it, a short reflection and who worked on it.

Can I run a project with a colleague?

Yes, with a Hub pass. Invite a colleague with a link; they give feedback and score their own subject's criteria.
