One project, four places

Potluck keeps it in one place: the plan, each group's board, the hand-ins, and the assessment at the end.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a project

    Start from a classroom-tested template, or fill in a short form with your own idea. Every project sits on the same frame: phases, milestones, roles, tasks and criteria.

  2. 2

    Fit it to your class

    Give your subjects, groups, time, level and language. AI rewrites the plan, the tasks and the criteria step by step, and you approve each step. Or run the template as it is.

  3. 3

    Run it on one board

    Students join with a code and a nickname. Groups claim tasks and hand in links to their work. You see who is late and who did what, then self, peer and teacher assessment close the project.

Questions

Do my students need accounts?

No. They type the project code and a nickname. Their phone stays signed in; a new device waits for your approval.

What happens to student data?

Potluck stores nicknames, task status, links to the work and the assessment answers. No e-mail addresses, no files. 90 days after you close the project the student data is deleted; you keep the plan.

Do students use AI in Potluck?

No. AI works only for you: when you fit a project to your class and, if you want, for written feedback at the end, which you edit before students see it.

What do students hand in?

A link to work made anywhere: a shared document, slides, a video, a map, a Canva page. With it, a short reflection and who worked on it.

Can I run a project with a colleague?

Yes, with a Hub pass. Invite a colleague with a link; they give feedback and score their own subject's criteria.
